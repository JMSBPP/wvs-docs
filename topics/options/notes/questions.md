
rationale:

The optimal fee is bounded below by the premium of the option for the LP as option seller and above by the arbitrageur's payoff. Both are functions of price returns over the in-the-money option interval and, more precisely, are dependent on volatility. This is different from the internal pricing rule; the optimal fee cannot be path dependent but needs to be controlled to be optimal for every trade on the path to the strike.
