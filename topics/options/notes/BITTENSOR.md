## MARKET

Let \(M^{(\tau)}(t)\) denote the monetary stock of the protocol token \(\tau\).

For each subnet \(K_i\), there is an associated subnet token:

$$
\alpha_i = \alpha(K_i).
$$

Therefore, each subnet is associated with a liquidity pool of the form:

$$
\left(M^{(\tau)}, \alpha_i\right). \, \leq (\bar M^{(\tau)}, \bar \alpha_i)
$$

The full collection of subnet liquidity pools is:

$$
\left\{\left(M^{(\tau)}, \alpha_i\right)\right\}_{i=1}^{N_{\text{sub}}}.
$$


$$
P_{\alpha_i}(t)
=
\nabla \, \varphi_i\left(\cdot; \Delta \alpha_i(t)\right).
$$

Thus, instead of taking the subnet-token price as exogenous, the model treats the pricing $\alpha$ is endogenously generated by the exogeneous pool mechanism.

### \(\varphi \, \equiv \text{CEMM}\)

$$
\begin{aligned}
	\varphi \, ( \eta \,;\alpha, M^{\tau}) \, &= \, \alpha^{\eta} \, M^{1-\eta} \, \implies \, \Delta P_{\alpha_i}(t)
=
\mu \, (\cdot \, , \eta ; P_{\alpha_i}(t) )
+
 \sigma_i (\cdot ,\, \eta ; P_{\alpha_i}(t)\, )\,\Delta W \, (t).

\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\Delta \, M_u^{(\tau)}(t)

=
\mu_F 
+
\sigma_F\,\Delta W \, (t),
$$

\[
	\sigma_i \, (\cdot ,\, \eta ; P_{\alpha_i}(t)\, ) \, = \, \delta \, P_{\alpha_i}^{\eta}
\]


This stochastic flow process is the primitive object driving endogenous subnet-token valuation.


$$
P_{\alpha_i}(t)
=
\frac{1-\eta}{\eta}
\frac{M^{(\tau)}(t)}
{\alpha_i(t)}.
$$

The induced volatility coefficient is


 $$
\delta
=
\frac{1}{1-\eta}
\left(
\frac{1-\eta}{\eta}
\right)^{1-\eta}
L^{-1}
\sigma_F.
$$

Therefore flow volatility \(\sigma_F\) is transmitted into subnet-token volatility through the market-maker elasticity.


The induced drift is

$$
\mu(P)
=
\frac{1}{1-\eta}
\left(
\frac{1-\eta}{\eta}
\right)^{1-\eta}
K^{-1}
\mu_F
P^{\eta}
+
\frac{\eta}{2(1-\eta)^2}
\left(
\frac{1-\eta}{\eta}
\right)^{2(1-\eta)}
K^{-2}
\sigma_F^2
P^{2\eta-1}.
$$

Consequently, both the first moment and second moment of subnet-token returns are ultimately determined by

$$
(\mu_F,\sigma_F,\eta).
$$

These three parameters completely characterize the endogenous subnet-token dynamics.


## USER ACTIONS : STAKE; UNSTAKE

The staking action is the user-side action in which the user deposits protocol token \(\tau\) and receives subnet token \(\alpha_i\).

Symbolically:

$$
\text{Stake:}
\qquad
\Delta M^{(\tau)}(t) > 0
\quad \longrightarrow \quad
\Delta \alpha_i(t) > 0.
$$

The unstaking action is the reverse operation, where the user deposits subnet token \(\alpha_i\) and receives protocol token \(\tau\).

Symbolically:

$$
\text{Unstake:}
\qquad
\Delta \alpha_i(t) < 0
\quad \longrightarrow \quad
\Delta M^{(\tau)}(t) < 0.
$$

The net user-side protocol-token flow is defined as:

$$
\Delta M^{(\tau)}(t)
=
\Delta M_{\text{stake}}^{(\tau)}(t)
-
\Delta M_{\text{unstake}}^{(\tau)}(t).
$$


---

## PROTOCOL ACTIONS: LIQUIDITY DEPOSITS


The protocol liquidity flow is a vector:

$$
\Delta L_i(t)
=
\left(
\Delta L_{\alpha_i}(t),
\Delta L_M^{(\tau)}(t)
\right).
$$

The protocol first determines the liquidity flow on the \(\tau\)-token side:

$$
\Delta L_{M^{(\tau)}}(\bar m, \underline{L_M}\, ;t)
=
\overline{m}
\,
\frac{
\max
\left\{
\operatorname{EMA}\left(\Delta M_u^{(\tau)}\right)(t)
-
\underline{L}_M,
0
\right\}
}
{
\displaystyle
\sum_{s \leq t} \max
\left\{
\operatorname{EMA}\left(\Delta M_u^{(\tau)}\right)(s)
-
\underline{L}_M,
0
\right\}
}.
$$

Then for a specific sub-net we have:

$$
\begin{aligned}
	\Delta L^i_{M^{(\tau)}}(\bar m, \underline{L_M}\, ;t) \, &= \, \frac{P_{\alpha_i} \, (t)}{\sum_j \, P_{\alpha_j}\, (t)} \, \Delta L_{M^{(\tau)}}(\bar m, \underline{L_M}\, ;t)
\end{aligned}
$$

Here:

- \(\Delta L_M^{(\tau)}(t)\) is the protocol-side liquidity injection in token \(\tau\).
- \(\overline{m}\) is the liquidity multiplier.
- \(\underline{L}_M\) is the lower-bound liquidity floor.
- \(\operatorname{EMA}(\Delta M_u^{(\tau)})(t)\) is the exponential moving average of net user stake-unstake flows.
- The denominator normalizes the halving-weighted history of flows.

---

After determining the protocol-token liquidity flow, the protocol computes the corresponding subnet-token liquidity flow using the endogenous subnet price as deflator:

$$
\Delta L_{\alpha_i}(t)
=
\min \bigg \{\frac{
1
}{
\sum_j \, P_{\alpha_j}\, (t)
} \,  \Delta L_{M^{(\tau)}}(t)\, , \, \Delta L_{\alpha_i}(t)
\bigg \}
$$

Therefore, the total protocol liquidity-expansion vector is:

$$
\Delta L_i(t)
=
\left(
\frac{\Delta L_M^{(\tau)}(t)}{P_{\alpha_i}(t)},
\Delta L_M^{(\tau)}(t)
\right).
$$


## PROTOCOL ACTIONS: TOKEN EMISSION
For each subnet \(K_i\), let

$$
\mathbf{T}=(T_j)_{j=1}^{N_T}
$$

denote a sequence of emission epochs.

The total stock of subnet token \(\alpha_i\) is decomposed into liquidity deposited in the pool plus cumulative emissions:

$$
\alpha_i(K_i;\mathbf{T})
\equiv
L_{\alpha_i}(\mathbf{T})
+
\sum_{j=1}^{N_T}
\mathcal{E}_{\alpha}(K_i;T_j).
$$

where

- \(L_{\alpha_i}(\mathbf{T})\) denotes liquidity held in the subnet pool,
- \(\mathcal{E}_{\alpha}(K_i;T_j)\) denotes token emissions distributed at epoch \(T_j\).

## PROTOCOL ACTIONS: STAKE DISTRIBUTION


At an emission epoch \(\bar T\), newly minted subnet tokens are distributed among protocol participants according to fixed protocol weights.

Let

$$
\bar w_m,
\qquad
\bar w_s,
\qquad
\bar w_o
$$

denote the aggregate weights assigned respectively to

- miners,
- stakers,
- subnet owner.

Then total emissions satisfy

$$
\mathcal{E}_{\alpha}(K_i,\bar T)
=
\left(
\bar w_m
+
\bar w_s
+
\bar w_o
\right)
\sum_{t=1}^{\bar T}
\Delta_t \mathcal{E}_{\alpha}(t).
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
	\rho \, (K_i; \cdot )\, &= \, \frac{\zeta \, M_{\tau} \, (K_i; \cdot)}{ \zeta \, M_{\tau} \, (K_i; \cdot) \, + \, \mathcal{E}_{\alpha}(K_i,\bar T)}
\end{aligned}
$$

for $\bar \underbrace{w_s}_{\text{validators}} = \, \rho \, (K_i; \cdot ) \, \underbrace{w_{sv}}_{\text{sub-net validators}}\, + \, (1\, -\, \rho \, (K_i; \cdot )) \, \underbrace{w_s}_{\text{root stakers}}$


The miner weight \(\bar w_m\) is itself distributed among individual miners according to a subnet-specific reward rule, which is left unspecified here.

Define the emission increment process

$$
\Delta_t \mathcal{E}_{\alpha}(t)
=
\frac{1}{t}
\mathcal{E}_{\alpha}(t-1).
$$

Positive values correspond to emission expansion while negative values correspond to emission contraction.

$$
\Delta M_u^{(\tau)}(t)
\longrightarrow
\Delta L_M^{(\tau)}(t)
\longrightarrow
\Delta L_{\alpha_i}(t)
\longrightarrow
\eta
\longrightarrow
\sigma \, (\eta)
\longrightarrow
P_{\alpha_i}(t; \sigma \, (\eta)
)
\longrightarrow
K_i.
\longrightarrow
P_{\pi \cdot} \, (\alpha ; \cdot)
$$


1. Users stake and unstake protocol token \(\tau\).
2. The net stake-unstake flow determines the protocol liquidity response.
3. The protocol injects liquidity first on the \(\tau\)-token side.
4. The protocol then computes the required subnet-token liquidity using the endogenous subnet-token price.
5. The subnet-token price becomes a valuation mechanism for subnet capital.
6. The resulting liquidity process supports capital formation across subnets.
